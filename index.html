Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

KeepBack is designed with privacy as a core principle. Your data stays on your device. We do not operate any servers, collect any analytics, or store any of your personal information.

Data We Access

When you connect Gmail, KeepBack requests read-only access to your Gmail account solely to identify purchase confirmation emails. We use this access to extract:

  • Merchant name and order details
  • Purchase amount and date
  • Return window information
  • Warranty information

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not store your email bodies or content
  • We do not transmit your purchase data to any server
  • We do not share your data with any third parties
  • We do not use analytics or tracking SDKs

Where Your Data Lives

All purchase records are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. Your Gmail OAuth tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain. Nothing is synced to iCloud or any external database.

Gmail Access

KeepBack uses Google's Gmail API with read-only scope. We never read, store, or transmit the contents of your emails beyond what is needed to identify purchase details in the moment of import. You can revoke access at any time from the app or from your Google Account settings.

Subscriptions

KeepBack Pro subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple via the App Store. KeepBack never receives or stores your payment information.

Children's Privacy

KeepBack is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.
