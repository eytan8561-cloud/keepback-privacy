Privacy Policy

KeepBack helps users track purchases, return deadlines, warranties, and related purchase information.

Information We Access

If you choose to connect Gmail, KeepBack requests read-only access to Gmail to identify purchase-related emails and extract purchase information such as merchant, order number, purchase amount, purchase date, return information, and warranty information.

Email Content

Gmail messages are fetched and processed on your device. KeepBack does not store complete email bodies or HTML content after processing. Only information extracted for your purchase records is retained.

Information Stored

Purchase information may be stored locally on your device, including product name, merchant, price, purchase date, return deadline, warranty expiration, order number, selected email message identifier, and limited purchase-related notes.

Where Your Data Goes

KeepBack does not operate a server or external database for your purchase information. Purchase data is stored locally on your device. Gmail authentication and Gmail data access are handled through Google's services.

Gmail Access

KeepBack uses Gmail's APIs only when you choose to connect your Gmail account. Gmail access is read-only and is used to identify and process purchase-related messages.

Authentication Information

Gmail authentication credentials and tokens are stored using Apple's Keychain services on your device. KeepBack does not receive or store your Google password.

Payments

Subscriptions and purchases are processed through Apple's App Store and StoreKit. KeepBack does not receive or store your payment card information.

Data Sharing

KeepBack does not sell your personal information or purchase information. KeepBack does not share purchase data with advertisers or data brokers.

Data Deletion

You can delete purchase records from the app. Disconnecting Gmail prevents KeepBack from accessing Gmail until you authorize it again.

Children

KeepBack is not intended for children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page.

Terms of Use

By using KeepBack, you agree to these Terms of Use.

Use of the App

KeepBack is provided as a tool for organizing purchase information and tracking return and warranty dates. Information extracted from emails may be incomplete or inaccurate. You are responsible for verifying important purchase, return, warranty, and refund information with the merchant.

Gmail Connection

You may optionally connect a Gmail account to allow KeepBack to identify purchase-related messages. You can revoke access through your Google account settings.

Subscriptions

KeepBack may offer paid subscription features. Subscriptions are billed through Apple's App Store according to the terms displayed at purchase.

No Guarantee

KeepBack does not guarantee that every purchase, return deadline, warranty, refund, price adjustment, or other information will be detected or displayed correctly.

Availability

KeepBack is provided on an "as is" and "as available" basis. Features may change, be interrupted, or become unavailable.

Contact

For questions about KeepBack, contact the developer through the support information provided in the App Store listing.
